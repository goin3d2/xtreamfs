Category changes at the provider

None seen since xtreamfs started.
Changes here are saved when you press Save and refresh, which starts a catalog refresh. Unticked categories are left out; a folder name gives a category its own top-level folder (and its own Plex/Emby library).
Settings saved here are stored in config/settings.json and take priority over .env. Use Reset to go back to the .env value. Your provider login stays in .env only. Settings marked restart need docker compose up -d (and a Plex restart) to take effect.
